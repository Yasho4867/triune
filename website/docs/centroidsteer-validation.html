## 1. Root-Cause Diagnosis: Why Legacy CentroidSteer Had Near-Zero Effect

Empirical and algebraic inspection of `triune/optim/centroid.py`, `triune/model/moe.py`, and `triune/kernels/fast_triangular.py` isolated **five compounding defects** in legacy CentroidSteer:

1. **Un-Deflated Krylov Subspace Absorption**: Legacy CentroidSteer ran randomized subspace iteration directly on the raw expert gradient $G$. Because $G = \sum_{x \in \mathcal{B}_e} g_y x^T$ already concentrates energy along the routed activation centroid $\mu_e$, the top-$r$ singular basis $Q_r$ absorbed the centroid direction. Orthogonalizing $c_e$ against $Q_r$ via $c_\perp = (I - Q_r Q_r^T) c_e$ left only low-energy orthogonal tail noise on the $(r+1)$-th steering border.
   - *Measured*: Residual centroid survival $\|c_\perp\|_2$ dropped to **0.1248** (legacy) vs. **1.0000** (upgraded), with border gradient energy fraction jumping **22.12x** (0.0139 -> 0.3082).
2. **Synthetic Hidden Centroid Mismatch ($w_2$ Experts)**: For `steer_role="hidden"`, legacy CentroidSteer approximated hidden activations by pushing the raw input centroid through the first layer (`FastGELU(W1 @ c_e)`), ignoring Jensen's inequality and non-linear variance.
   - *Measured*: Cosine similarity between synthetic `FastGELU(W1 @ c_e)` and true post-activation $\mathbb{E}[\text{FastGELU}(W_1 x)]$ was **0.9897** (relative L2 error **0.1437**).
3. **Non-Contrastive Raw Centroids Under Shared Residual Offset**: Legacy raw EMAs $\mu_e$ captured shared Transformer residual-stream DC offsets rather than expert-specific specialization.
   - *Measured*: Off-diagonal inter-expert centroid cosine similarity collapsed from **0.5763** (raw) to **-0.3329** (contrastive $\Delta \mu_e = \mu_e - \bar{\mu}$).
4. **Stale `subspace_step` Bias-Correction Damping**: Whenever the steering border rotated every `steer_update_gap` steps and zeroed border moments $(m, v)$, legacy CentroidSteer continued dividing by $1 - \beta_1^{\text{subspace\_step}}$ instead of resetting a dedicated `steer_step` counter, damping border updates immediately after refresh.
   - *Measured*: At `subspace_step=10, steer_step=1`, legacy border update was damped to **0.4844** vs. **1.0000** with independent `steer_step` (**2.06x** recovery).
5. **Rank-1 Bottleneck**: Legacy steering injected only a single 1D direction per expert without multi-directional subspace coverage or out-of-core activation drift correction between `update_gap` refreshes.

## 2. Upgraded CentroidSteer Architecture (R2)

- **True Post-Activation Hidden & Multi-Directional Subspaces (`triune/model/moe.py`)**: `MoE_FFN` captures true `last_hidden_centroids`, computes `last_contrastive_centroids` and `last_hidden_contrastive_centroids`, and maintains $k_c=4$ orthonormal activation subspace directions (`last_input_subspace`, `last_hidden_subspace`) via two-pass modified Gram-Schmidt (`_orthonormalize_subspace_batch`) in both batched BMM and fallback paths.
- **Centroid-Deflated Krylov + Seeded Probes (`triune/optim/centroid.py`)**: Subspace refresh deflates $G$ along $\hat{c}_e$ before Krylov iteration (`G_defl`) and seeds probe columns with secondary activation directions, preserving 100% of $\hat{c}_e$ for column $r+1$ while capturing non-centroid curvature in $Q_r$.
- **Independent `steer_step` & Border RMS Regularization (`triune/kernels/fast_triangular.py`)**: Triton kernel & PyTorch fallback apply independent `steer_step_vals` bias correction on row/column $r+1$ and border RMS variance regularization (`rms_reg=0.35`), paired with sign-continuity alignment (`last_c_orth`).
- **Fisher-Alignment-Weighted Multi-Directional & Live Out-of-Core Steering (`triune/optim/centroid.py`)**: Combines utilization-weighted border scaling, in-core $k_c$-directional subspace boost, and scale-preserving live orthogonal drift correction (`live_ortho_updates`) between `update_gap` refreshes.
