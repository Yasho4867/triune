Triune Architecture (TriuneTransformer)

Constant-memory O(1) recurrent Gated Linear Attention (VectorisedGLA, 64.0 KiB state cache up to L=16,384 tokens, 99.8% cache reduction vs. Softmax SDPA), Gated DeltaNet (100% MQAR recall), Sparse Scatter-Gather MoE, 3-tier Reflex/Limbic/Cortex hierarchical early exits with Conformal CALM calibration and self-speculative decoding, and Activation-Checkpointed LayerStreamingEngine training 1.52B models inside an 8 GB consumer GPU.

CentroidSteer 3-Tier Partitioned Optimizer

Tri-tier partitioned manifold optimization combining Muon Newton-Schulz orthogonalization (Tier 1), CentroidSteer Block-Oja activation-subspace steering with sCholQR2 retraction and Grassmann parallel transport on Stiefel manifolds (Tier 2, 0.1118 B/p / 98.4% reduction vs. FP32 AdamW, validated across 5 seeds), and base AdamW (Tier 3).

Junkie Model Family
